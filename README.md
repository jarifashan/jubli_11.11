# A Little World for Jubli — 11:11

A small, mobile-friendly, eight-slide appreciation website for Jubli. The colours, sunflower details, panda and sloth illustrations are woven into the design as subtle visual touches rather than presented as a list of favourites. It uses built-in CSS and simple SVG drawings, so it does not need stock images, AI-generated photos, external libraries, or a build step.

## Preview on your computer

1. Download the project ZIP and extract it.
2. Open the `jubli_1111_site` folder.
3. Double-click `index.html`. It should open in your browser.

## Add your own sky photos

1. Choose up to three sky photos from your phone or computer.
2. Copy them into the `photos` folder.
3. Rename them exactly to `sky-1.jpg`, `sky-2.jpg`, and `sky-3.jpg`.
4. Keep the names lowercase and make sure the file extension matches `.jpg`.
5. Refresh the website. The pastel placeholders will be replaced by your photos.

If your photos are PNG files, either convert them to JPG or change the matching filename in `index.html` from `.jpg` to `.png`.

## Publish with GitHub Pages (beginner steps)

1. Sign in at https://github.com/ or create a GitHub account.
2. Click the **+** button at the top-right, then choose **New repository**.
3. Name it something like `jubli-1111`.
4. Choose **Public**, then click **Create repository**.
5. Open the new repository. Click **Add file** → **Upload files**.
6. Open the extracted project folder on your computer. Upload `index.html`, `README.md`, and the `photos` folder (including your renamed photos, if you added them).
7. Click **Commit changes**.
8. Open the repository's **Settings** → **Pages**.
9. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
10. Choose branch **main** and folder **/(root)**, then click **Save**.
11. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish. The website address will appear in the Pages section. It usually looks like `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/jubli-1111/`.

## Personal edits

Open `index.html` in a text editor such as Notepad. Search for the words you want to change. Your name is currently written as `Jarif` in the two sign-off places. Edit those words if you'd prefer a different signature.

To change the sky captions, search for `A little pause`, `A little warmth`, and `A little wonder`.

To change any letter or comfort message, search for the sentence on the matching slide or in the `comfortMessages` section near the bottom of the file.
